Flows

  1. Open settings: right-click the pill, or its settings button if on.
  2. Change style: click a card. The pill swaps live and resizes, right edge fixed.
  3. Hide the button: flip the switch. Right-click keeps working.
  4. First run: default style, button on, so the way in is visible.
